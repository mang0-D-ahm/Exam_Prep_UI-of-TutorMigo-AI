PASSAGE · URBAN ECOLOGY

Small spaces, richer cities

Community gardens are often described as modest green spaces, yet their value reaches beyond the food they produce. In dense neighborhoods, even a small plot can provide habitat for pollinators and create a shared place where residents meet.

Researchers have also found that gardens can improve environmental awareness. When people observe seasonal changes firsthand, they are more likely to support broader conservation efforts. The strongest projects pair access to land with local knowledge and long-term stewardship.

“The garden is not an isolated patch of green; it is a point of connection.”

CRAFT & STRUCTURE

A material that remembers

Shape-memory alloys can return to a predetermined form after being deformed and heated. Their behavior comes from a reversible change in crystal structure, allowing engineers to design compact components that respond to temperature.

Because the effect is repeatable, these materials are used where conventional motors would be too large or mechanically complex.

Exam prep/IELTS Academic/Test format

IELTS EXAM GUIDE · FORMAT

Know the four test sections

IELTS Academic and General Training share the same Listening and Speaking tests.

Test formatTest typesBand scoresExam day

WRITTEN TEST · ABOUT 2 HOURS 30 MINUTES

Listening, Reading, and Writing

01Listening · about 30 minutesFour parts and 40 questions. On paper tests, an additional 10 minutes is provided to transfer answers; computer-test arrangements differ.
02Reading · 60 minutesThree sections and 40 questions. Passages differ between Academic and General Training.
03Writing · 60 minutesTwo tasks. The prompts differ by test type, and Task 2 carries more weight than Task 1.

SPEAKING TEST · 11–14 MINUTES

A short, face-to-face interview

01Part 1 · IntroductionAnswer questions about familiar topics such as home, study, or interests.
02Part 2 · Individual long turnSpeak on a topic card after a short preparation period.
03Part 3 · DiscussionExplore broader questions related to the Part 2 topic.

Speaking may be scheduled on the same day or separately. Confirm the timing in your test booking.

Exam prep/IELTS Academic/Test types

IELTS EXAM GUIDE · TEST TYPES

Choose the right IELTS test

Check the exact test and score requirements with the organization you are applying to.

Test formatTest typesBand scoresExam day

FOR HIGHER EDUCATION AND PROFESSIONAL REGISTRATION

IELTS Academic

Academic is commonly used for entry to undergraduate or postgraduate study and for some professional registration. Its Reading passages and Writing tasks assess skills used in academic settings.

01ReadingUses a range of descriptive, factual, and analytical texts.
02Writing Task 1Describe and summarize visual information, such as a chart, graph, table, or process.

FOR MIGRATION, WORK, OR BELOW-DEGREE STUDY

IELTS General Training

General Training focuses on everyday, workplace, and social contexts. It may be accepted for migration or other purposes, depending on the receiving organization.

01ReadingUses texts found in everyday life, workplace settings, and general-interest contexts.
02Writing Task 1Write a letter in response to a given situation.

Listening and Speaking are the same in both test types. Always verify which test your institution, employer, or immigration authority requires.

Exam prep/IELTS Academic/Band scores

IELTS EXAM GUIDE · SCORING

Understand your band score

IELTS reports individual skill scores and an overall band on a 0–9 scale.

Test formatTest typesBand scoresExam day

FOUR SKILL SCORES

One band for each skill

01ListeningScore reflects the number of correct answers, converted to a band score.
02ReadingScore reflects correct answers; Academic and General Training use different conversion scales.
03Writing and SpeakingExaminers assess performance against criteria including task achievement, coherence, vocabulary, and grammar or fluency and pronunciation.

OVERALL BAND

How the overall result is calculated

The overall band is the average of the four skill scores, rounded to the nearest whole or half band according to IELTS rounding rules.

01There is no universal pass or failEach institution or authority sets its own required overall and skill scores.
02Check every requirementSome applications require a minimum band in each skill as well as an overall score.

DIGITAL SAT GUIDE · FORMAT

Know the test structure

The SAT is taken digitally in Bluebook and is divided into two subject sections.

Test formatScoresExam day

ABOUT 2 HOURS 14 MINUTES OF TESTING

Reading and Writing · 64 minutes

01Two adaptive modulesEach module is 32 minutes, with a 10-minute break after Reading and Writing.
0254 questions totalShort passages are paired with questions on information and ideas, craft and structure, expression, and grammar.

MATH · 70 MINUTES

Two adaptive modules

0144 questions totalQuestions cover algebra, advanced math, problem-solving and data analysis, and geometry and trigonometry.
02Calculator availableAn approved calculator or the built-in Bluebook calculator may be used throughout Math.

Performance in the first module of each section helps determine the difficulty of the second module.

Test formatScoresExam day

TOTAL SCORE · 400–1600

Two section scores

01Reading and Writing · 200–800One section score based on performance across both adaptive modules.
02Math · 200–800One section score based on performance across both adaptive modules.

HOW TO USE YOUR RESULT

Compare scores with your goals

The digital SAT uses adaptive testing, so scoring accounts for question difficulty as well as correct answers. There is no penalty for guessing.

01Check your target schoolsScore ranges and policies vary by institution and application year.
02Review the full score reportUse skill details to identify areas for practice, not just the total score.

GRE GENERAL GUIDE · FORMAT

Know the GRE structure

The GRE General Test takes about 1 hour 58 minutes, excluding check-in and instructions.

Test formatScoresExam day

ANALYTICAL WRITING · 30 MINUTES

One “Analyze an Issue” task

Develop and support a clear position in response to an issue prompt. There is one essay task in the current GRE General Test.

01Plan a clear argumentAddress the issue, develop reasons and examples, and organize ideas logically.
02Write and reviseLeave time to check clarity, sentence structure, and mechanics.

VERBAL AND QUANTITATIVE REASONING

Two sections for each measure

01Verbal ReasoningTwo sections: 12 questions in 18 minutes, then 15 questions in 23 minutes.
02Quantitative ReasoningTwo sections: 12 questions in 21 minutes, then 15 questions in 26 minutes.
03Section-level adaptationYour performance in the first section of a measure affects the difficulty of its second section.

Verbal and Quantitative · 130–170
